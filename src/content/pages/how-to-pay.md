Before you start: prepare for identity verification (KYC) Mercuryo uses identity verification (Know Your Customer, or KYC). Be ready to complete the checks it requests before your purchase can proceed; requirements depend on your country and transaction. Valid government-issued ID Have the original document accepted for your country ready: a passport, national ID card, or driving licence where supported. Mercuryo's current guide requires at least three months before expiry. US users must use a valid US driver's licence or state-issued ID, not a US passport. Camera and a well-lit space Use a phone or computer with a working camera for document photos and a selfie/liveness check when prompted. Allow camera access. Keep all document corners visible and your face unobstructed; do not use edited photos or filters. Personal details and email access Have your full legal name, date of birth, and any address or additional details Mercuryo requests ready. They must match your ID. Keep access to your email for the verification code. US users should also be ready to provide their SSN to Mercuryo during its verification. Debit card and bank authentication Use a debit card. Keep the card details and access to your bank's app or other authentication method ready for any payment confirmation it requests. Check that your balance covers the card charge, including Mercuryo's fees. Provide ID, selfies, card details, and other sensitive verification information only through Mercuryo's official flow or its verification provider reached from that flow. Do not send them to PFC.
