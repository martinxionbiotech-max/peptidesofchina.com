Refund and cancellation terms for **china peptide** orders.

We operate on a request-first basis with no processing deposit. The cancellation point is the final payment. Before that point, you may cancel without cost.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

## Before the final invoice is accepted

You may cancel before accepting and paying the final invoice. Because no processing deposit is collected, there is nothing to refund at this stage — you simply do not proceed. If requested items are unavailable, the invoice is adjusted before final payment, and you approve the adjusted invoice before paying.

## After final payment

Final payment confirms the order. Customer-requested cancellation or refund is no longer available. If order assembly has not started, you may ask to add items; any addition is subject to availability, confirmation, and supplemental payment. Supplier-side nonperformance is handled under the resolution commitments below.

## Delivery and product problems

Loss, return, customs, and confirmed damage issues are handled through replacement or reshipment under the [Shipping & Reshipment Policy](/shipping-and-reshipment-policy/). They do not automatically create a cash-refund right.

## Supplier-side nonperformance

If Peptides of China confirms that a supplier cannot fulfill a fully paid public-catalog order as agreed, Peptides of China will arrange an appropriate resolution. This may include completion of the order, agreed alternative fulfillment, or a refund.

Customer-requested cancellation and carrier or customs events remain subject to their respective policies.

[Learn about Peptides of China's role in order support and resolution.](/company/)

## Approved refunds

When a refund is approved, it is processed manually within a reasonable time after approval. For blockchain payments, the original asset and network are normally used. Additional verification may be required before any refund destination is accepted.

## Eligibility and timeline

Refunds and cancellations are reviewed individually against the order status at the time of the request:

| Request timing | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Before final invoice | Cancel freely — no deposit, nothing charged |
| After final payment | Order confirmed; no customer-requested cancellation |
| Stock unverifiable | Invoice adjusted to confirmed items only |
| Supplier nonperformance | Replacement, alternative fulfillment, or refund |
| Carrier/customs events | Reshipment per Shipping & Reshipment Policy |

**Disputes:** where a supplier does not meet its confirmed obligations, Peptides of China manages the resolution, including alternative fulfillment or a refund where appropriate. Reviews are processed in the order received; typical response times are stated on the invoice or in the corresponding correspondence.
