Refund and cancellation terms for **china peptide** orders.

The cancellation point is the final payment. Before that point, the processing deposit can be returned or retained as client credit.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

## Before the final invoice is accepted

You may cancel before accepting and paying the final invoice. The processing deposit may be returned or, at your choice, recorded as client credit for future use. If requested items are unavailable, the invoice can be adjusted before final payment.

## After final payment

Final payment confirms the order. Customer-requested cancellation or refund is no longer available. If order assembly has not started, you may ask to add items; any addition is subject to availability, confirmation, and supplemental payment. Supplier-side nonperformance is handled under the resolution commitments below.

## Delivery and product problems

Loss, return, customs, and confirmed damage issues are handled through replacement or reshipment under the Shipping & Reshipment Policy. They do not automatically create a cash-refund right.

## Supplier-Side Nonperformance

If Peptides of China confirms that a supplier cannot fulfill a fully paid public-catalog order as agreed, Peptides of China will arrange an appropriate resolution. This may include completion of the order, agreed alternative fulfillment, or a refund.

Customer-requested cancellation and carrier or customs events remain subject to their respective policies.

[Learn about Peptides of China’s role in order support and resolution.](/company/)

## Approved refunds

When a refund is approved, it is processed manually within a reasonable time after approval. For blockchain payments, the original asset and network are normally used. Additional verification may be required before any refund destination is accepted.

## Eligibility & Timeline

Refunds and cancellations are reviewed individually against the order status at the time of the
request. Requests submitted before stock verification or production are generally eligible for
cancellation; orders already in production or dispatch follow supplier-side resolution terms.

**Disputes:** where a supplier does not meet its confirmed obligations, Peptides of China manages
the resolution, including alternative fulfillment or a refund where appropriate. Reviews are
processed in the order received; typical response times are stated on the invoice or in the
corresponding correspondence.
