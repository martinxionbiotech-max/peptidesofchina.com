Peptides of China publishes research-use-only material for laboratory buyers, procurement teams and researchers. The five guides below are the entry points to the site's cluster content. They are not a product catalogue and they are not medical advice.

## Start with the question you actually have

Most buyers arrive with one of five questions:

1. **Can I verify the supplier before I pay?** → [China Peptide Sourcing](/china-peptide-sourcing/)
2. **What does the COA actually prove?** → [Peptide Quality & Testing](/peptide-quality-testing/)
3. **How do I handle the vial once it arrives?** → [Peptide Handling & Research Protocols](/peptide-handling-protocols/)
4. **What is this compound class, and what is it not?** → [Peptide Categories & Mechanisms](/peptide-categories-mechanisms/)
5. **Where is the research-use boundary?** → [Peptide Compliance & Sourcing Risk](/peptide-compliance-risk/)

## How the five pillars fit together

- **Sourcing** is commercial: who makes the batch, what documents travel with it, how it ships, and what happens when it does not arrive.
- **Quality & Testing** is analytical: identity, purity, counterions, endotoxins, sterility and batch consistency.
- **Handling & Protocols** is laboratory practice: reconstitution, storage, freeze-thaw, adsorption and measurement.
- **Categories & Mechanisms** is the research map: GHRH/GHRP, GLP-1, cosmetic, bioregulator and mitochondrial peptides, with preclinical context only.
- **Compliance & Risk** is the boundary: research-use-only claims, import documentation, supplier representation and the claims a seller should not make.

## What these guides are not

They are not dosing guides. They do not describe human use, injection schedules, cycling, or therapeutic outcomes. Peptides of China sells research compounds for in-vitro and preclinical work; the guides are written to help buyers evaluate sourcing and laboratory handling, not to recommend anyone use a compound.

## Editorial standard

Each pillar follows the same structure: direct answer, key facts, decision framework, failure modes, source list, and a labelled `## The Author's Take`. The stance is editorial judgment about sourcing and evidence quality — not a medical claim.

Where the evidence is thin, the page says so. Where suppliers make claims that cannot be verified from the batch record, the page says that too.

## Cluster pages

The pillars link to the existing blog corpus and to product pages. Start with a pillar, then use its cluster links to go deeper. If a product page and a pillar disagree, the pillar's sourcing/quality framework takes precedence; the product page is a listing, not a monograph.

**Research use only.** Nothing on this page is medical advice, a dosing instruction, or a claim of human efficacy.
