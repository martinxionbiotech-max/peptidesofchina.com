Custom peptide services extend our **china peptide** catalog to sequences not listed in stock.

Turn a product concept into a production-ready research peptide with help from Peptides of China.

## What We Can Create

Custom research compounds, peptide blends, cap colors, vial presentation, and private-label vial labels are reviewed and quoted for each project.

## How Your Custom Project Works

1. Tell us what you want to build.
2. We review it with the manufacturer.
3. You receive a clear proposal.
4. Specification and artwork are approved.
5. Peptides of China manages production and shipping.

## Quality and Documentation

Available manufacturer documentation can be included in the proposal, and third-party testing can be arranged separately with a recognized laboratory.

## Research Use Only

Custom compounds and blends supplied through Peptides of China are intended for laboratory research only and are not sold for human or veterinary use.
