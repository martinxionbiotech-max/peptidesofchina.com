This page explains how **china peptide** orders move from catalog selection to delivery.

We operate on a request-first basis with no processing deposit. One box is enough to start. Stock is verified directly with synthesis facilities before the final invoice is issued.

## 01 — Build & Submit Request

**No payment yet.**

Browse the catalog and add compounds to your request. Provide your contact details and submit. This is a sourcing request — not a purchase. No payment and no deposit are collected at submission.

## 02 — Stock Verification

**Direct facility check.**

After your request is submitted, we check real-time availability directly with the relevant synthesis facility. This is a manual process — we do not draw from pooled inventory. Verification and the supplier's response typically take about 2 business days (China time, GMT+8).

## 03 — Final Invoice & Shipment

**Pay the invoice, order ships.**

Once stock is confirmed, you receive a final invoice. If any items are unavailable, the invoice is adjusted accordingly. After final payment, processing, assembly, and handover to the carrier take 2–3 business days — Express transit time is counted from that handover.

## 04 — Delivery Support & Resolution

**Support continues after dispatch.**

Our involvement continues after dispatch. We monitor delivery progress and coordinate applicable reshipments, replacements, and supplier-side order issues. If a supplier does not meet its confirmed obligations, Peptides of China manages the resolution, including alternative fulfillment or a refund where appropriate.

## Shipping & Customs Policy

Shipping is $97–127 per parcel, Express only, with an estimated transit time of 7–12 days counted from handover to the carrier. The final shipping cost is confirmed based on the supplier, destination, and parcel count before final payment. Customs handling times are outside our control.

- **International Shipping:** Shipping is $97–127 per parcel, Express. Estimated transit is 7–12 days, counted from carrier handover. Final cost depends on the supplier, destination, and parcel count. Customs handling times remain outside our control.
- **Customs Returns & Reship:** We monitor shipment progress and act when tracking confirms a loss, return to sender, or customs problem; you do not need to submit a separate reshipment request when we have already identified the issue. Qualifying Shanghai Labs shipments have unlimited reshipments until delivery. Qualifying Shenzhen shipments receive one complimentary reshipment. Other situations are reviewed individually.
- **Invoice Adjustment:** If any items are unavailable after stock verification, the final invoice is adjusted to reflect only confirmed items. You approve before paying.

## Key Principles

- **No-deposit processing:** Requests start without any processing deposit. One box is enough to place a first order.
- **Manual verification:** Each request is individually handled. Stock is checked directly with the relevant synthesis facility, not from pooled inventory.
- **Adjusted final invoice:** Your final invoice reflects only what is actually confirmed and available. Nothing is substituted without notice.
