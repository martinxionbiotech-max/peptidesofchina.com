# Pre Orders

PRE-ORDERS NOW AVAILABLE — CUSTOM-MANUFACTURED RESEARCH COMPOUNDS We have launched a new Pre-Order system for custom-manufactured research compounds. Currently available for US clients only, the program includes 22 specialized compounds, including bioregulatory peptides, P21 (Adamantane), PE-22-28, Larazotide, amidate versions of Epithalon/Selank/Semax, and other difficult-to-source research compounds We are excited to announce the launch of our new Pre-Order system for custom-manufactured research compounds. At the moment, 22 different compounds are available through the program, including a broad range of bioregulatory peptides as well as several highly unique and difficult-to-source compounds such as: • P21 (Adamantane) • Larazotide • NA Epithalon Amidate • NA Selank Amidate • NA Semax Amidate • and many others. Unlike standard catalog products, these compounds are manufactured specifically to order at our partner facility in Hunan Province. HOW THE PROCESS WORKS Place Your Order Once your pre-order is submitted and confirmed, 100% upfront payment is required in order to initiate manufacturing. Manufacturing Phase Production begins immediately after payment confirmation. Estimated manufacturing time: ~10 business days International Transit After production is completed, the shipment is sent from Hunan to our US receiving facility. Estimated transit time: ~15 days Domestic Delivery Orders are then dispatched from our US location directly to the client. Estimated domestic delivery: ~5 business days Estimated total timeline from order confirmation: ~30 days IMPORTANT DETAILS • Pre-Orders are currently available for US clients only. • Some compounds are available starting from just 1 box, while others require higher minimum quantities depending on manufacturing requirements. • Standard minimum order value of $600 still applies. • Access to the Pre-Order system is restricted to active clients only. To request access, please contact us through the contact form on the website in order to receive the password required to unlock the Pre-Order section. This system allows us to provide access to highly specialized research compounds that are often unavailable through traditional inventory-based sourcing models. For research purposes only.
