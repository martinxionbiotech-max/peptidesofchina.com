# Shipping Expansion

Shipping Expansion: India, South Africa, and Nigeria Now Available We are pleased to announce that products from our Shanghai Labs manufacturing partner are now available for delivery to India, South Africa, and Nigeria. We are pleased to announce another expansion of our international shipping network. Products manufactured by our partner, Shanghai Labs, are now available for delivery to: India South Africa Nigeria Shanghai Labs currently manufactures virtually all products available in our regular catalog, meaning that the vast majority of our standard product lineup can now be shipped to customers in these countries. Important information for customers in India: Due to local customs and regulatory requirements, the delivery address must exactly match the address listed on the recipient’s government-issued government ID. Orders shipped to an address that does not match the registered ID may experience customs clearance delays or be rejected by local authorities. We strongly recommend verifying your shipping information before placing an order to ensure a smooth delivery process. Customers in South Africa and Nigeria can place orders using our standard ordering and shipping process without any additional country-specific requirements. We remain committed to expanding our global shipping coverage and will continue adding new destinations as reliable logistics routes become available. Thank you for choosing PeptidesFromChina.co .
