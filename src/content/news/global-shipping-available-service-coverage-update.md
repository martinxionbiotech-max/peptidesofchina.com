# Global Shipping Available Service Coverage Update

Global Shipping Available — Service Coverage Update We continue to process and ship orders worldwide, with a few regional exceptions. We are pleased to confirm that PeptidesFromChina provide international shipping to clients worldwide. Orders are being processed and delivered across the majority of regions without interruption. At the same time, due to logistical and regulatory considerations, we are currently unable to ship to the following countries and regions: Brazil India Pakistan Middle East Africa This limitation is based on shipping reliability and customs-related factors, and allows us to maintain a consistent level of service quality for our clients. We continue to monitor these regions and may adjust our coverage as conditions evolve. If you are unsure whether delivery is available to your location, please feel free to contact us via the feedback form. We appreciate your understanding and remain committed to providing stable and reliable service worldwide.
