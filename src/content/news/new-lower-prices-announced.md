PFC has completed a new pricing agreement with one of its manufacturing partners, allowing us to reduce prices across a substantial selection of research products. The updated prices are effective immediately, with reductions exceeding 20% on certain catalog positions . The revised pricing has already been applied to the relevant products in our public catalog. Product specifications and existing quality-control requirements remain unchanged. This update reflects our continuing commitment to providing competitive prices, reliable sourcing, and access to an expanding selection of research compounds.
