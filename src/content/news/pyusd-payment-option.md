# Pyusd Payment Option

PYUSD Now Available as a Payment Option Home › News › PYUSD Now Available as a Payment Option Announcement 2026-08-31 PYUSD Now Available as a Payment Option PFC now accepts PayPal USD (PYUSD) on the Solana network, providing customers with an additional stablecoin payment option. PYUSD Now Available as a Payment Option PFC now accepts PayPal USD (PYUSD) as an additional payment method. PYUSD is a U.S. dollar-backed stablecoin issued by Paxos for PayPal and designed to maintain a 1:1 value with the U.S. dollar. In supported markets, PYUSD can be purchased directly through PayPal and transferred to compatible external wallets. PYUSD is also supported by Phantom Wallet, making it accessible to users within the Solana ecosystem. PFC currently accepts PYUSD on the Solana network only. Customers using PYUSD should ensure that the payment is sent via Solana to the designated wallet address provided during the payment process. The availability of PYUSD purchase, transfer, and withdrawal features through PayPal may vary by country.
