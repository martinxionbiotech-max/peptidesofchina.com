# Hgh Supply Export Update

HGH Supply & Export Update Increasing export compliance requirements and logistics restrictions continue affecting HGH shipments from China. PFC remains committed to maintaining stable access for professional researchers through direct manufacturing partnerships. Over the past months, international HGH shipments from China have become significantly more complicated due to increasing export compliance requirements, customs scrutiny, and logistics restrictions affecting the industry. As a result, many suppliers and trading companies have either suspended HGH exports entirely or experienced substantial disruptions in shipping operations and product availability. PFC continues to operate with a strong focus on the B2B segment, professional researchers, and direct cooperation with manufacturers. HGH remains one of the most important and widely utilized compounds in the research space, and we continue working closely with manufacturing partners to maintain stable access despite the evolving regulatory and logistics environment. At this time, HGH products from our partner Beijing Biotech are available under a dedicated fulfillment process. Please note: • Minimum order quantity: 3 boxes (30 vials) • HGH orders are shipped separately from standard orders • Additional export handling and compliance procedures may affect processing times We appreciate your patience, understanding, and continued trust. — PFC Team
