## Hot New Arrival: SLU-PP-915 Tablets Are Now Available

The Peptides of China catalog continues to grow.

We have recently added several new research compounds, bringing our public catalog to **more than 140 unique products**. One of the most exciting additions is **SLU-PP-915**, now available in tablet form.

SLU-PP-915 belongs to a new generation of synthetic compounds being studied for their ability to activate estrogen-related receptors ERRα, ERRβ, and ERRγ. These nuclear receptors play an important role in the regulation of mitochondrial function, cellular energy utilization, fatty-acid oxidation, and oxidative metabolism.

Despite their name, estrogen-related receptors are not classical estrogen receptors and are not activated by estrogen.

**What Makes SLU-PP-915 Different from SLU-PP-332?**

Both SLU-PP-915 and SLU-PP-332 are pan-ERR agonists, meaning that they activate all three major ERR isoforms. They should not, however, be viewed simply as different versions or strengths of the same compound.

**SLU-PP-332** is the earlier and more extensively studied research compound. Preclinical studies in mice have associated it with activation of exercise-related metabolic pathways, increased energy expenditure, enhanced fatty-acid oxidation, improved exercise capacity, and reduced fat accumulation.

**SLU-PP-915** is a chemically distinct compound developed using a different molecular scaffold. Its primary advantage is oral activity: it was specifically developed to improve drug-like properties and has demonstrated oral bioavailability and in-vivo activity in animal research.

In simple terms:

- **SLU-PP-332** is the earlier research prototype with a larger body of preclinical metabolic data.
- **SLU-PP-915** is a newer, chemically distinct pan-ERR agonist developed as an orally active research candidate.

Recent preclinical research found that orally administered SLU-PP-915 enhanced aerobic exercise capacity and activated gene pathways associated with mitochondrial and oxidative metabolism in mice.
