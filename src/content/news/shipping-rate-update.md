Peptides of China — our platform for **china peptides** sourcing — publishes short updates like this one whenever something material changes: catalog additions, quality-process notes, and sourcing announcements. The substance of this update is below.


Standard Line Shipping Rate Update Due to increasing logistics complexity and the growing use of multi-stage transportation routes involving several carriers and intermediate transit hubs, Standard Line shipping rates have been updated effective immediately. Dear Customers, Due to the increasing complexity of international logistics operations, which now frequently involve multiple transportation partners, intermediate transit hubs, and multi-stage delivery routes, we are updating our Standard Line shipping rates. Effective immediately: Standard Line shipping for the first 0.5 kg has increased from $50 to $55 Each additional 0.5 kg has increased from $10 to $15 These changes reflect the rising operational and logistics costs required to maintain stable and reliable delivery channels under current market conditions. We remain committed to providing secure international shipping and maintaining the highest possible delivery reliability for our customers worldwide. Thank you for your understanding and continued support.
