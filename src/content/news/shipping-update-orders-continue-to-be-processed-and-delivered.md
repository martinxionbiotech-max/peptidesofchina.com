# Shipping Update Orders Continue To Be Processed And Delivered

Shipping Update: Orders Continue to Be Processed and Delivered Despite recent changes in customs regulations, all orders are being processed and shipped as usual through our stable delivery channels. Due to recent tightening of customs regulations affecting international shipments, many vendors across the industry have encountered significant disruptions, including delays and, in some cases, an inability to dispatch orders. In response to these changes, we have made adjustments to our logistics chain to ensure continuity and maintain reliable service for our clients. At this time, FedEx express delivery is temporarily unavailable . However, our specialized vial delivery line remains fully operational and stable . All orders continue to be processed and shipped through this channel without interruption. We are closely monitoring the situation and working to restore full shipping flexibility as soon as possible. We appreciate your understanding and will keep you informed of any further updates in a timely manner.
