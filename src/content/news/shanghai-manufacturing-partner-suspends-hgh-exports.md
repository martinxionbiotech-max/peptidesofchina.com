# Shanghai Manufacturing Partner Suspends Hgh Exports

Shanghai Manufacturing Partner Suspends HGH Exports Home › News › Shanghai Manufacturing Partner Suspends HGH Exports Announcement 2026-05-08 Shanghai Manufacturing Partner Suspends HGH Exports We have received official notice from one of our manufacturing partners in Shanghai that HGH products can no longer be exported. All HGH-related products have been temporarily marked as out of stock while we finalize agreements with two additional large-scale manufacturers in China. New catalogs — including HGH, bioregulators, and advanced research compounds — are expected soon. We would like to inform our customers that we have just received official notice from one of our manufacturing partners in Shanghai that they are no longer able to export HGH products. As a result, all HGH-related products on our platform have been temporarily marked as Out of Stock. At the same time, we are currently in the final stage of negotiating agreements with two additional large-scale manufacturing facilities in China. Once these agreements are finalized and their product catalogs become available, we expect to significantly expand our offering — including a broad selection of bioregulators, advanced and hard-to-source research compounds, as well as HGH products. We will publish additional updates as soon as the new supply channels are fully integrated into our system. We appreciate your understanding and continued support.
