As part of our mid-2026 catalog review, the following SKUs have been retired from our wholesale offering, effective immediately.

## HGH Fragment 176-191 (FR2, FR5)

Fragment 176-191 in both the 2mg (FR2) and 5mg (FR5) formats has been discontinued.

AOD-9604, a modified and stabilized analog based on the C-terminal region of human growth hormone, remains available as an alternative for research within the same category.

AOD-9604 is available in our catalog under Weight Management.

## MOTS-c 20mg (MS20)

The 20mg vial format of MOTS-c has been discontinued.

MOTS-c remains available in the following formats:

• MOTS-c 10mg (MS10) — $69/box
• MOTS-c 40mg (MS40) — $207/box
