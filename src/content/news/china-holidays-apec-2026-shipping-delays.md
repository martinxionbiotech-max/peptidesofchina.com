# China Holidays Apec 2026 Shipping Delays

Important Shipping Advisory: China Holidays & APEC 2026 Home › News › Important Shipping Advisory: China Holidays & APEC 2026 Announcement 2026-08-19 Important Shipping Advisory: China Holidays & APEC 2026 Upcoming public holidays in China and the APEC Summit in Shenzhen may affect order processing and international shipping between late September and November. Customers are encouraged to place orders early to minimize potential delays. Important Shipping Advisory for September–November 2026 We would like to give all PFC customers advance notice of several upcoming events in China that may affect order processing and international shipping. Key Dates Mid-Autumn Festival: September 25–27, 2026 National Day / Golden Week: October 1–7, 2026 APEC Summit in Shenzhen: November 18–19, 2026 During the September and October holiday periods, many factories, offices, warehouses and logistics providers in China operate with reduced staff or temporarily suspend operations. Shipping volumes also typically increase significantly before the holidays and immediately after operations resume, which can create additional backlog throughout the logistics network. Potential APEC Shipping Disruptions The 2026 APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting will take place in Shenzhen on November 18–19. Freight partners are preparing for the possibility of enhanced security measures and stricter cargo inspections in Shenzhen and surrounding logistics hubs before and during the summit. At this stage, it is impossible to predict the exact impact on individual shipping routes, but additional processing time and temporary disruptions are possible. We Recommend Ordering Early If you are planning to place an order during September, October or November , we strongly recommend ordering earlier than usual. For orders you would like processed before the October holidays, we recommend placing them by mid-September whenever possible . For November orders, we recommend placing them before early November and allowing additional time for international delivery. PFC will continue working with our suppliers and logistics partners to minimize disruptions and keep orders moving whenever possible. We will post additional updates if the situation changes or if specific shipping routes are affected.
