# Product Catalog Update August 2026

Product Catalog Update: Discontinued and Newly Added Products Home › News › Product Catalog Update: Discontinued and Newly Added Products Announcement 2026-08-04 Product Catalog Update: Discontinued and Newly Added Products Several products have been discontinued, while PEG-MGF, PE22-28, Adamax, and MOTS-c 20 mg have been added to our catalog. Product Catalog Update We have updated our product catalog with several new research compounds and removed a number of discontinued products. Discontinued Products Shanghai Labs has discontinued the following products, which have now been removed from our catalog: T100 — Tirzepatide 100 mg CBL60 — Cerebrolysin 60 mg GTT600 — Glutathione 600 mg GTT1200 — Glutathione 1200 mg GTT1500 — Glutathione 1500 mg AD2 — AOD-9604 2 mg AD10 — AOD-9604 10 mg BF70 — GLOW Blend with TB-500 Fragment 17–24 KF80 — KLOW Blend with TB-500 Fragment 17–24 AOD-9604 in the 5 mg strength remains available in our catalog. Removal of Cerebrolysin and Glutathione The removal of Cerebrolysin and Glutathione is related to their regulatory classification. These products are registered pharmaceutical drugs and were previously supplied as finished pharmaceutical products. Due to increasingly strict regulatory requirements governing this category, they can no longer be distributed within the framework used for research substances and chemical reagents. This decision is related exclusively to regulatory and supply requirements and is not connected to any concerns regarding product quality. Newly Added Products The following products have been added to our catalog and are now available for ordering: FMP2 — PEG-MGF 2 mg 22-10 — PE22-28 10 mg AX — Adamax 10 mg, 984 Da version MS20 — MOTS-c 20 mg The current availability and pricing for each product can be found directly in the product catalog.
