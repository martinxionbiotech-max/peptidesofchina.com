Peptides of China — our platform for **china peptides** sourcing — publishes short updates like this one whenever something material changes: catalog additions, quality-process notes, and sourcing announcements. The substance of this update is below.

Peptides of China continues to focus on the B2B segment, professional researchers, and direct manufacturer partnerships. As part of this transition, the minimum order amount has been updated from $300 to $600.

Peptides of China continues to develop as a platform focused on the B2B segment, professional researchers, and direct cooperation with manufacturers.

As part of this direction, the minimum order amount has been updated from $300 to $600.

Most large-scale manufacturers we work with are focused on long-term partnerships and larger supply volumes rather than small individual orders. This update will allow us to maintain stable cooperation with manufacturing partners, support a broader product range, and ensure more efficient order processing.

We appreciate your understanding and continued trust.

— Peptides of China Team
