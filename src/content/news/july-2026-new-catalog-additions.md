## TB-500 Fragment (17–24) — New Compound & Fragment Blend Series

TB-500 Fragment (17–24) is now available as a standalone compound and as the basis for a new Fragment variant of our existing blend series.

Derived from the actin-binding region of Thymosin Beta-4, the Fragment provides a more focused research option centered on the biological activities associated with this region, including cell migration, tissue repair, and angiogenic signaling. It is also significantly more affordable than the full 43-amino acid Thymosin Beta-4 molecule.

The two formats are intended to serve different research objectives. When the research focus is specifically on the activity associated with the actin-binding region, TB-500 Fragment (17–24) offers a more targeted and cost-effective option. When a broader spectrum of Thymosin Beta-4 biological activity is required, the full-length 43-amino acid molecule — and the Wolverine Blend, GLOW, and KLOW formulations based on it — remains the more comprehensive option.
