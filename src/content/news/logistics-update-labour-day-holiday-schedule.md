# Logistics Update Labour Day Holiday Schedule

Logistics Update: Labour Day Holiday Schedule | Peptides of China Home › News › Logistics Update: Labour Day Holiday Schedule Announcement 2026-04-27 Logistics Update: Labour Day Holiday Schedule Shipping is operating normally (~15 days), but will be paused from April 29 to May 5 due to Labour Day Holiday in China. Place your orders in advance to avoid delays. We would like to inform our customers that our logistics operations are currently running normally, with an average delivery time of approximately 15 days . Please note that due to the upcoming Labour Day Holiday in China , shipping will be temporarily suspended from April 29th through May 5th . All operations will resume as usual after the holiday. During this period, most factories and logistics providers in China will be closed, which may impact order processing and shipment timelines. We strongly recommend planning your orders in advance and placing them ahead of the holiday to avoid any delays in inventory replenishment.
