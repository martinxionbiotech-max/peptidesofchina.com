# Us Warehouse Inventory Expanded

More Than 30 Products Now Available from Our U.S. Warehouse Home › News › More Than 30 Products Now Available from Our U.S. Warehouse Announcement 2026-09-20 More Than 30 Products Now Available from Our U.S. Warehouse PFC has significantly expanded its U.S. warehouse inventory. More than 30 products are now available to customers across the United States with domestic delivery in approximately 5–7 business days. PFC is pleased to announce a significant expansion of its U.S. warehouse inventory. More than 30 products are now stocked domestically and available for delivery throughout the United States. Orders fulfilled from our U.S. warehouse normally arrive within approximately 5–7 business days. Because the products are already located inside the country, customers benefit from: Faster domestic delivery No international transit or customs clearance Reduced risk of cross-border shipping delays Continued order fulfillment during the holiday closure in China Convenient access to a substantially broader selection of locally stocked products This expansion allows our U.S. customers to continue placing and receiving orders even while manufacturing facilities, warehouses, and international logistics operations in China are temporarily closed for the holiday period. Current availability is displayed directly in the PFC catalog. To order an eligible product from local stock, select the U.S. Warehouse fulfillment option on the product page.
