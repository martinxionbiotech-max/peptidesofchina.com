Peptides of China — our platform for **china peptides** sourcing — publishes short updates like this one whenever something material changes: catalog additions, quality-process notes, and sourcing announcements. The substance of this update is below.

Peptides of China has significantly expanded its U.S. warehouse inventory. More than 30 products are now available to customers across the United States with domestic delivery in approximately 5–7 business days.

Peptides of China is pleased to announce a significant expansion of its U.S. warehouse inventory. More than 30 products are now stocked domestically and available for delivery throughout the United States.

Orders fulfilled from our U.S. warehouse normally arrive within approximately 5–7 business days. Because the products are already located inside the country, customers benefit from:

- Faster domestic delivery
- No international transit or customs clearance
- Reduced risk of cross-border shipping delays
- Continued order fulfillment during the holiday closure in China
- Convenient access to a substantially broader selection of locally stocked products

This expansion allows our U.S. customers to continue placing and receiving orders even while manufacturing facilities, warehouses, and international logistics operations in China are temporarily closed for the holiday period.

Current availability is displayed directly in the Peptides of China catalog. To order an eligible product from local stock, select the **U.S. Warehouse** fulfillment option on the product page.
