# Pfc Minimun Order

PFC Launches One-Month No-Minimum-Order Trial Home › News › PFC Launches One-Month No-Minimum-Order Trial Announcement 2026-09-18 PFC Launches One-Month No-Minimum-Order Trial PFC has temporarily removed the $600 minimum order requirement. For the next month, customers may submit requests of any value, provided products are ordered in complete boxes. PFC Launches One-Month No-Minimum-Order Trial Following feedback from our community, PFC has launched a one-month trial with no minimum order value . Effective immediately, the previous $600 minimum order requirement has been removed. Customers may submit requests of any value, with the only remaining restriction being that products must be ordered in complete boxes. Individual vials cannot be supplied separately. During the trial period, we will evaluate order volume, processing requirements, and operational efficiency. At the end of the month, a final decision will be made on whether to retain the new policy, modify it, or restore a minimum order requirement.
