Peptides of China — our platform for **china peptides** sourcing — publishes short updates like this one whenever something material changes: catalog additions, quality-process notes, and sourcing announcements. The substance of this update is below.


New Feature Released: Back-in-Stock Notifications for Unavailable Items You can now subscribe to out-of-stock items and receive an email notification as soon as they become available again. We’ve introduced a new feature designed to improve the user experience and make tracking product availability easier. From now on, if a specific item is temporarily out of stock, you can add it to your notification list. Once the product becomes available again, you will automatically receive an email alert. This feature is especially useful for high-demand compounds that may have limited availability or are restocked in batches. Our goal is to eliminate the need for manual checking and ensure that you don’t miss the moment when the product you’re interested in becomes available again. To use this feature, simply select an out-of-stock item and enable the notification option. The system will take care of the rest. We will continue to expand platform functionality based on user feedback and real usage patterns. More updates coming soon.
