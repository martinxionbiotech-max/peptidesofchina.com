Peptides of China — our platform for **china peptides** sourcing — publishes short updates like this one whenever something material changes: catalog additions, quality-process notes, and sourcing announcements. The substance of this update is below.

We are pleased to announce the beginning of our cooperation with Guangdong Bio.

Guangdong Bio is a relatively young manufacturer, but we began building our relationship with them around six months ago. During this time, the manufacturer has been highly responsive to our recommendations regarding process organization, operational standards, and the requirements needed to meet our criteria.

We are now glad to share that our final inspection confirmed that their processes meet the standards required for us to offer products from the Guangdong Bio catalog to our clients.

This manufacturer has particularly strong capabilities in the production of various blends. They were also among the first to establish high-quality in-house production of Eloralintide, which is now available for ordering as well.

At the moment, we have made 16 Guangdong Bio products available for order, and we plan to expand the available assortment further.
