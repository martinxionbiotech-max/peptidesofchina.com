# China Holiday Shipping Update

China Holiday Shipping Update: September 24–October 8 Home › News › China Holiday Shipping Update: September 24–October 8 Announcement 2026-09-17 China Holiday Shipping Update: September 24–October 8 PFC will continue accepting and processing new requests throughout the upcoming holiday period in China. Due to warehouse closures and heavy congestion across shipping channels, some orders may not be dispatched until October 9 or later. China Holiday Schedule: Order Processing and Shipping Update China’s Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holiday period will run from September 24 through October 8. Due to heavy pre-holiday demand, limited air-cargo capacity, and temporary closures across warehouses and logistics providers, some orders submitted over the coming days or during the holiday period may not be dispatched until October 9 or later. The PFC support team and designated production managers will remain available throughout the holiday. We will continue to: Accept new order requests Answer client questions Review submitted requests Place confirmed requests into the fulfillment queue Warehouses and logistics departments will not operate during the holiday period, so physical order assembly and shipment will resume once they reopen. All requests are processed in the order in which they are received. If you are planning to place an order, we recommend submitting your request in advance. This will secure an earlier position in the fulfillment queue when normal operations resume. Requests submitted after October 8 will be processed after those received before and during the holiday. We expect order-processing times to return to their normal schedule within approximately 5–7 business days after the holiday ends and the accumulated backlog has been cleared. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience and understanding.
