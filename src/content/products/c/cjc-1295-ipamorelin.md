## Compound Profile

The CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin combination is the most widely studied GH secretagogue co-administration protocol in preclinical research. The two compounds act at distinct, mechanistically complementary receptors: CJC-1295 (modified GRF 1-29) targets the GHRH receptor (GHRHR) on anterior pituitary somatotrophs, while Ipamorelin targets the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) at both pituitary and hypothalamic levels. Simultaneous engagement of both receptor systems produces synergistic GH pulse amplification that exceeds what either agent achieves alone — a well-characterized finding in rodent pituitary research models. Research interest in this combination centers on the mechanistic synergy between GHRHR and GHS-R1a pathways. At the cellular level, GHRHR signals via Gs-cAMP-PKA, while GHS-R1a signals via Gq-PLC-PKC; dual activation of both pathways within the same somatotroph cell is hypothesized to produce multiplicative rather than additive GH exocytosis. Ipamorelin's selectivity profile — high GH specificity with minimal cortisol, prolactin, or FSH stimulation at research doses — makes it a preferred GHS-R1a agonist for combination protocols where minimizing off-target secretagogue effects is important.
