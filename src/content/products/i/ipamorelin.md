## Compound Profile

Ipamorelin was developed as a selective GH secretagogue rather than a broad ghrelin mimetic, and that design intent still explains most of its research use. ## Why This Compound

The GH axis has two input channels that are easy to confuse. GHRH neurons deliver a receptor-level signal through GHRHR on somatotrophs; ghrelin-type ligands act through GHSR-1a on the same cells and on hypothalamic circuits. Ipamorelin sits in the second channel. It binds GHSR-1a, a Gq/G11-coupled receptor whose activation raises inositol trisphosphate and mobilizes intracellular calcium, and that calcium surge is what drives secretory granule fusion in the somatotroph.

What separates ipamorelin from GHRP-6 and GHRP-2 is the shape of the downstream response. In the Raun paper that introduced the compound, ipamorelin released GH from rat pituitary cells and from anesthetized rats, while ACTH and prolactin stayed close to baseline. GHRP-6, by contrast, is a ghrelin receptor agonist with a broader endocrine footprint. For a study that wants to attribute a result to GHSR-1a rather than to a stress-axis or lactotroph side effect, that difference matters more than a decimal place of potency.

The five-residue sequence is also an experimental convenience. Aib at position 1 and D-2-naphthylalanine at position 3 are not natural amino acids, so the peptide is not a fragment of an endogenous protein and is unlikely to be mistaken for one in an antibody-based assay. The C-terminal amide removes the negative charge that a free carboxylate would add, which tends to improve receptor binding and slows carboxypeptidase attack. There is no cysteine, methionine or tryptophan in the chain, and no asparagine or glutamine either. That profile is worth remembering when you interpret stability data: the usual oxidation and deamidation pathways have little to work with here.

Preclinical use clusters in four areas. Primary rat anterior pituitary cultures are the standard in vitro system for measuring GH release and for testing whether a response survives a GHRHR antagonist. Intact rodent models are used for pulse profiling, where serial sampling shows whether a compound produces a discrete secretory episode or a sustained plateau. GH-deficient and aged rodents appear in work on somatotroph reserve, because their blunted endogenous output makes an evoked response easier to detect. Outside the pituitary, GHSR-1a is expressed in the enteric nervous system and in vagal afferents, so gastric-emptying and motility endpoints show up in the literature as a separate line of inquiry.

None of that makes ipamorelin a substitute for a GHRH analogue. The two receptors converge on the same secretory machinery through different second messengers, which is why combination designs exist. A GHRHR agonist supplies the cAMP arm; a GHSR-1a agonist supplies the calcium arm. Used together in rodent models, the GH response is larger than either input alone. ## What the Batch Record Should Show

A batch record for a pentapeptide is short, but it should be complete. The first item is identity, and for ipamorelin the intact mass of 711.85 Da is the quickest check. A single mass peak at the expected value tells you the main component is the right chain length; it does not tell you the sequence is correct. For that you need MS/MS fragmentation covering all five residues, including the non-natural Aib and D-2-Nal. A supplier who reports only "MW confirmed" without a spectrum has verified less than the phrase suggests.

Purity comes next. Reverse-phase HPLC at 214 nm is the standard peptide-bond wavelength, and the report should state the column, gradient, wavelength and integration threshold. A main-peak percentage is an area percentage, not a mass fraction. If the record claims 99% purity, ask what the other 1% is: water, acetate, residual trifluoroacetic acid, or peptide-related impurities. Those are different things and they affect a study differently. Water content by Karl Fischer is the single most neglected number on peptide certificates; a lyophilized cake that reads 98% peptide by HPLC can contain several percent moisture by mass.

Counterion identity deserves its own line. Ipamorelin is typically supplied as the acetate salt because acetate is exchanged during preparative HPLC and is acceptable for most assay systems. Residual trifluoroacetic acid from purification is a separate matter. TFA can persist in the lyophilized powder, and at sufficient concentration it shows up as a cytotoxic effect in cell culture that has nothing to do with the peptide. If your assay uses sensitive cells, ask for residual TFA and acetate content by ion chromatography rather than accepting "peptide purity" alone.

The rest of the record is standard but should not be assumed: fill mass per vial, cake appearance and color, pH of the reconstituted solution, endotoxin by LAL, and bioburden or sterility if the vial is sold as sterile. A specification without an acceptance criterion is a description, not a test. The document should carry a lot number that ties to the vials in the box, the manufacturer's name and site, the test methods used, the results, and a retest or expiry date.

Because ipamorelin has no methionine, cysteine or tryptophan, oxidation is a minor pathway, and because it has no asparagine or glutamine, deamidation is not the usual degradation route. The impurities you should expect are hydrolysis fragments, dimers and higher aggregates, and possibly sequence-related by-products from synthesis. Size-exclusion chromatography or SDS-PAGE under non-reducing conditions will show aggregation that RP-HPLC can miss. If the batch record contains only one HPLC trace, the analytical package is thinner than the molecule's failure modes.

## Sourcing & Verification

The useful questions are specific, and the answers are checkable. Who synthesised the lot, at which site, and was it made in-house or bought from a second factory? What was the crude purity before purification, and how many preparative HPLC passes were used? Which counterion was exchanged in, and what is the residual TFA level? Can the supplier send the actual chromatogram file rather than a table of numbers? Does the certificate belong to the lot in the box, and does the lot number appear on the vial label as well as the paper?

Ask for a retention sample as well. A supplier who keeps sealed vials from each lot for two years is operating a quality system; a supplier who cannot produce one is reselling. Ask whether every lot is tested or whether the certificate is a periodic qualification of the process. Both practices exist, but they mean different things, and the difference should be visible in the document.

Price is a weak signal in isolation, but the pattern matters. A quote far below the market for a 5 mg vial usually reflects one of three things: lower fill mass, lower purity with a generous integration window, or a trading company that never sees the goods. ## Handling & Storage

Lyophilized ipamorelin should be held at -20°C, desiccated and protected from light. Let the vial equilibrate to room temperature before opening it. A cold vial in a warm room condenses moisture on the inside of the septum and on the powder, and that moisture is the beginning of every later stability problem. Wipe the septum, open deliberately, and do not leave the vial open longer than the transfer requires.

For reconstitution, use a solvent matched to the assay. Sterile water is adequate for immediate in vitro work; a buffered saline is better when pH control matters; bacteriostatic water is common when a vial will be sampled repeatedly, though the preservative itself can interfere with some cell assays. Add the liquid down the wall of the vial rather than onto the cake, swirl gently, and give it time. Vortexing a peptide solution creates interfacial stress and promotes aggregation, which is exactly what you do not want before a binding experiment.

Concentration affects recovery. Dilute peptide solutions adsorb to polypropylene and, more strongly, to glass and some filter membranes. If you plan a nanomolar working solution, prepare a concentrated stock first and dilute into a buffer containing a carrier protein such as bovine serum albumin for cell work. Filtering a dilute solution through a membrane can remove a meaningful fraction of the peptide; filter the buffer before the peptide is added, or use a low-binding filter.

Aliquot before freezing. A vial that is thawed and refrozen five times has a different aggregation profile from one thawed once, and the change is not visible. Hold working aliquots at -80°C for long-term storage, keep the reconstituted solution at 4°C for no more than the 14 days stated in the specification, and discard anything that turns cloudy or shows particulate matter. ## How It Compares

Against [GHRP-6](/catalog/ghrp-6/), ipamorelin trades some raw secretory power for selectivity. GHRP-6 is the older tool, well represented in the literature, and it brings ghrelin-like effects on food intake and on the corticotroph axis that can confound a GH-focused design. Ipamorelin's flatter ACTH and prolactin profile is the reason many current protocols prefer it.

Against [GHRP-2](/catalog/ghrp-2/), the contrast is similar but less stark. GHRP-2 is a potent GHSR-1a agonist with a longer practical record in combination work, and it does raise prolactin in several models. A lab that needs the strongest possible evoked GH signal may still choose it; a lab that needs a clean attribution will not.

Against [hexarelin](/catalog/hexarelin/), the difference is pharmacological rather than analytical. Hexarelin has been studied for effects outside the pituitary, including cardiac tissue, and it shows more pronounced desensitisation on repeated exposure in some models. That makes it a specialised tool, not a default.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is ipamorelin?**

Ipamorelin is a synthetic pentapeptide that acts as a selective agonist at GHSR-1a, the ghrelin receptor. Its sequence is Aib-His-D-2Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH₂, and it is used in laboratory research on somatotroph function and GH-axis signaling. It is not a medicine and is not supplied for human or veterinary use.

**How does ipamorelin differ from GHRP-6?**

Both bind GHSR-1a, but GHRP-6 also produces measurable ACTH, cortisol and prolactin responses in preclinical models and has stronger effects on feeding behavior. Ipamorelin was selected for a narrower endocrine footprint, which makes it easier to attribute an observed GH response to GHSR-1a activation alone.

**Is your ipamorelin an acetate salt?**

The catalog material is supplied as the acetate salt, which is the usual counterion for synthetic peptides purified by preparative HPLC. Residual trifluoroacetic acid from purification is controlled separately. If your assay is sensitive to either ion, request the ion-chromatography data for the lot before ordering.

**What should a certificate of analysis include for ipamorelin?**

It should show the lot number, manufacturer and site, intact mass, an MS/MS spectrum covering the full sequence, HPLC method and purity result, water content, acetate and residual TFA values, fill mass, appearance, endotoxin result, and a retest date. A certificate that lists only "purity ≥98%" is not enough to distinguish a good lot from a mediocre one.

**How should lyophilized ipamorelin be stored?**

Keep it at -20°C in a desiccated container, protected from light, and let the vial reach room temperature before opening. Once reconstituted, use it within 14 days at 4°C. Freeze single-use aliquots at -80°C if the work runs longer.

**Can ipamorelin be used in cell-based assays?**

Yes. GHSR-1a-expressing cell lines and primary rat anterior pituitary cultures are the standard formats. Calcium mobilisation and GH-release assays are common readouts. Keep working solutions in a carrier-protein-containing buffer to limit adsorption losses, and confirm that the solvent has no effect on the vehicle control.

**Why is ipamorelin often paired with a GHRH analogue?**

The two receptors use different second-messenger pathways. GHRHR activation raises cAMP; GHSR-1a activation raises intracellular calcium. Activating both in the same somatotroph produces a larger secretory response than either alone in rodent models, which is the rationale for the combination designs described above.
