## Compound Profile

Ipamorelin is a synthetic pentapeptide (five amino acids) that acts as a selective agonist at the ghrelin receptor (GHSR-1a), classified as a growth hormone releasing peptide (GHRP). Its sequence — Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH₂ — includes non-natural amino acid substitutions (Aib at position 1, D-2-naphthylalanine at position 3) that confer receptor selectivity and resistance to peptidase degradation relative to earlier GHRPs such as GHRP-6 and GHRP-2. Preclinical research characterises ipamorelin primarily through its stimulation of pulsatile growth hormone release from anterior pituitary somatotrophs via GHSR-1a activation and secondary GH-releasing hormone (GHRH) synergism.
