The peptide is harder to pin down. That does not prove the peptide does not exist. It does mean the catalogue's mechanism claim is not yet backed by a public, citable dataset that a laboratory can inspect.

This page therefore does two things at once. It explains the FKBP5 biology that makes the claimed target interesting, and it sets out what a buyer should demand before treating PE-22-28 as an FKBP51 reagent rather than an unidentified heptapeptide.

## Why This Compound

FKBP51 is a co-chaperone in the Hsp90 machinery that assembles the glucocorticoid receptor. It binds the receptor through its FK506-binding domain and reduces the receptor's sensitivity to cortisol. When FKBP51 is abundant, glucocorticoid receptor nuclear translocation is less efficient and the negative feedback loop that normally shuts down the stress response is weaker. The consequence in cells is a longer cortisol signal after stress, not a stronger one.

The human genetics are unusually consistent for a psychiatric target. A 2008 study in *JAMA* reported that FKBP5 polymorphisms interacted with childhood abuse to predict post-traumatic stress disorder symptoms in adults. A 2013 study in *Nature Neuroscience* showed allele-specific DNA demethylation at the FKBP5 locus after childhood trauma, providing a molecular mechanism for a gene-environment interaction. A 2020 study in the *Journal of Clinical Investigation* linked the glucocorticoid receptor–FKBP51 complex to fear conditioning and PTSD-like behaviour in mice. These papers are about the target, not about PE-22-28, and that distinction matters.

Spinorphin, the claimed parent, is an endogenous heptapeptide first isolated from bovine spinal cord. It inhibits enkephalin-degrading enzymes and has been studied in pain and inflammation models. Its sequence is seven residues long. The catalogue describes PE-22-28 as "residues 22–28" of spinorphin, which cannot be right if spinorphin is itself a heptapeptide: a seven-residue peptide has no position 22. The numbering more plausibly refers to the hemoglobin β-chain from which spinorphin is derived, or to an internal fragment of a larger precursor. That is exactly the kind of definitional ambiguity a buyer should resolve before ordering.

The proposed mechanism — FKBP51 inhibition releasing the glucocorticoid receptor from a low-affinity state — is coherent and testable. It is also not demonstrated for this peptide in any paper we could locate. The methods that would demonstrate it are standard: surface plasmon resonance or isothermal titration calorimetry for binding affinity, a competition assay against the FKBD1 domain, and a glucocorticoid receptor translocation assay in a cell line with known FKBP51 expression. A supplier who has run those experiments should be able to produce the reports.

## What the Batch Record Should Show

The certificate should print the full seven-residue sequence, the calculated mass, the observed mass, and the charge states. A nominal "MW ~880" is a round number, not a measurement. For a peptide this short, a one-residue substitution changes the mass by 1–130 Da depending on the residue, and a missing C-terminal amide changes it by about 1 Da. The observed mass should be reported to two decimal places and compared with a calculated value for the declared sequence.

Purity by HPLC needs a method that can resolve the peptide from its truncation products. A seven-residue peptide has many possible one-residue deletions, and several of them will elute close to the parent on a shallow gradient. The certificate should show the gradient, the column, the wavelength and the integration parameters. If the chromatogram is a single peak with no scale on either axis, it is an illustration, not evidence.

Counterion and peptide content are the next fields. A 10 mg vial at this price contains peptide plus counterion plus residual water; the peptide fraction may be 70–85% of the fill. The certificate should name the counterion (acetate or trifluoroacetate) and give Karl Fischer water content. A peptide-content assay, whether by amino acid analysis or nitrogen determination, converts the fill into active mass. Without it, every molar concentration in the study is approximate.

Endotoxin testing matters for any peptide used with primary cells or in vivo. Ask whether the lot was tested by LAL, what the limit was, and whether the result is lot-specific. Sterility does not follow from lyophilization; it is a separate, lot-specific test. The [quality pillar](/peptide-quality-testing/) explains why these are separate release decisions.

If the supplier claims FKBP51 binding, ask for the binding data. A certificate of analysis is not a pharmacology report, and the two should not be conflated. The binding report should state the method, the protein construct, the buffer, the temperature, the fitted Kd with a confidence interval, and the positive control. A single "Kd = 2 µM" line without those details is not reproducible. The [testing guide](/blog/peptide-testing-and-analysis-a-professional-guide-to-laboratory-verification/) covers the difference between identity testing and functional testing.

Batch traceability closes the record. A matching lot number, a named site and a synthesis date are the minimum traceability fields. For a peptide with an uncertain literature footprint, traceability is the only way to compare one batch with the next.

## Sourcing & Verification

Start with the sequence. Ask the supplier to write out all seven residues, the terminal modifications, and the calculated mass. If the answer is "PE-22-28, 98%," you have learned that the supplier is selling a catalogue name rather than a defined molecule.

Ask what the "22–28" refers to. A supplier who understands the product will explain the parent numbering; a reseller will repeat the marketing copy. The answer is a useful test of whether anyone in the chain has seen a sequence file. The [manufacturer verification protocol](/blog/china-peptide-manufacturer-verification-a-2026-b2b-sourcing-protocol/) covers the entity checks that should come first.

Ask for the binding report if FKBP51 is the reason you are buying. The report should include the FKBD1 construct, the assay format, the controls, and the raw binding curves. If the supplier has no report, the honest description is "heptapeptide of unconfirmed target," and the price should reflect that. Do not accept a citation to a paper that studies a different molecule.

Ask whether the peptide is supplied as the free acid or the amide, and whether the N-terminus is acetylated. These details change the mass and the charge and are often omitted from catalogue pages. A supplier who cannot answer has not characterised the batch.

Ask about the lot history. If the product is made to order, the first lot may be the only lot, and there is no batch-to-batch dataset. That is acceptable for exploratory work but should be priced and planned accordingly. The [sourcing pillar](/china-peptide-sourcing/) covers lead times and documentation for international orders.

One caution about the category label. PE-22-28 is listed on the neuro shelf, a category that groups peptides by commercial positioning rather than by mechanism. FKBP51 biology is neuroendocrine, and the category label tells you nothing about whether the peptide engages it. ## Handling & Storage

Store the powder at −20°C, desiccated and protected from light. Equilibrate the vial to room temperature before opening. A cold vial attracts condensation, and a short peptide with a hydrophobic face can aggregate when it takes up water.

PE-22-28 should dissolve in water or phosphate-buffered saline if the sequence is sufficiently polar. If it does not, dissolve in a small volume of DMSO and dilute into buffer, keeping the final organic concentration as low as the assay allows. Run a vehicle control at the same DMSO concentration. Avoid heating and vortexing; both promote aggregation and adsorption.

For binding assays, the buffer matters more than the concentration. Salt, pH, detergent and carrier protein can all change a weak interaction. Use the buffer reported in the assay you are trying to reproduce, and record it. If you are establishing a new assay, run a concentration series and a no-protein control before interpreting a signal.

Aliquot before freezing. Aqueous solutions are short-term stocks at 2–8°C; repeated freeze-thaw cycles promote aggregation and loss to the container. Aliquot into single-use volumes, store at −20°C or below, and avoid a second freeze-thaw. See the [reconstitution guide](/blog/peptide-lyophilization-reconstitution-guide-for-researchers/) for the mechanics and the [handling pillar](/peptide-handling-protocols/) for the wider workflow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is FKBP51 and why is it a research target?**

FKBP51 is a co-chaperone encoded by the FKBP5 gene. It binds the Hsp90–glucocorticoid receptor complex and reduces the receptor's sensitivity to cortisol, weakening the negative feedback that ends the stress response. FKBP5 variants that increase FKBP51 expression have been associated with PTSD risk and impaired stress recovery in genetic studies. The target is well validated biologically; that does not validate any particular peptide as a ligand.

**Is PE-22-28 a fragment of spinorphin?**

The catalogue says it corresponds to residues 22–28 of spinorphin. Spinorphin is a seven-residue peptide, so numbering within spinorphin cannot reach position 22. The numbering may refer to the hemoglobin β-chain or to a larger precursor. Ask the supplier to explain the convention and to print the sequence.
