## Compound Profile

Hexarelin (Examorelin) is a synthetic hexapeptide GH secretagogue and GHS-R1a agonist, structurally related to GHRP-6 but with a 2-methyl-tryptophan (α-methyltryptophan) at position 2 — a modification associated with the highest reported GH-releasing potency among the hexapeptide GHRP family. Beyond its pituitary effects, hexarelin is notable in research for cardiovascular activity mediated via CD36 (scavenger receptor B) and a distinct GHS-R1a-independent pathway, making it a pharmacologically complex research tool that spans GH axis and cardiac biology. Preclinical studies have documented hexarelin's robust GH stimulation in rodent and primate models, generally exceeding the GH release amplitude achieved by GHRP-6 or GHRP-2 at equivalent doses. A separate line of research has examined hexarelin's cardioprotective properties in ischemia-reperfusion injury models — an area of interest because the observed cardiac effects appear to persist even in somatostatin-suppressed or hypophysectomized animals, implicating a GH-independent receptor mechanism (CD36) in cardiac signaling.
