## Compound Profile

HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin) is a purified gonadotropin preparation derived from the urine of post-menopausal women, containing a mixture of FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone) and LH (luteinizing hormone) activity in approximately equal proportions. Post-menopausal urine contains elevated gonadotropin concentrations due to loss of ovarian feedback inhibition on the hypothalamic-pituitary axis. HMG preparations are standardized by bioassay to defined FSH and LH activity units, providing a clinically and experimentally relevant preparation that reproduces the combined gonadotropin stimulation required for full gonadal function. Research applications for HMG center on reproductive endocrinology, gonadal physiology, and fertility biology. In female models, FSH stimulates follicular development (granulosa cell proliferation, estradiol production, follicle growth), while LH triggers ovulation and luteinization. HMG provides simultaneous FSH and LH stimulation, which more closely models physiological gonadotropin co-stimulation than individual recombinant preparations. Rodent superovulation protocols using HMG followed by hCG (human chorionic gonadotropin — LH analogue) are standard research designs for producing large numbers of oocytes for assisted reproduction and developmental biology research.
