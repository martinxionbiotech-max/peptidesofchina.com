## Frequently Asked Questions

How does AICAR activate AMPK?**

AICAR enters cells and is phosphorylated by adenosine kinase to ZMP (AICA ribonucleotide), which structurally resembles AMP. ZMP allosterically binds to the CBS regulatory domains of the AMPK β and γ subunits, mimicking the high-AMP state of energy-depleted cells. This activates AMPK's kinase domain — the same mechanism by which elevated cellular AMP activates AMPK during exercise or caloric restriction. 

**How does AICAR differ from metformin as an AMPK activator?**

Metformin activates AMPK indirectly via mitochondrial Complex I inhibition, which raises the AMP/ATP ratio and secondarily activates AMPK. AICAR activates AMPK directly via ZMP accumulation without affecting mitochondrial function. AICAR is preferred in research when direct AMPK activation without mitochondrial confounds is required.

## Compound Profile

AICAR (5-Aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleotide), also known as Acadesine or AICA riboside, is a cell-permeable nucleoside analogue and pharmacological activator of AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK). After cellular uptake, AICAR is phosphorylated to ZMP (AICA ribonucleotide) — a structural analogue of AMP — which allosterically activates AMPK by mimicking the energy-depleted state (high AMP/ATP ratio). AMPK is the master sensor of cellular energy status, regulating metabolic switching, mitochondrial biogenesis, glucose uptake, and fatty acid oxidation across virtually all tissues. Preclinical research using AICAR as a pharmacological AMPK activator spans metabolic research, exercise physiology, cardiac protection, and cancer biology. In rodent models, AICAR administration produces effects resembling endurance exercise: increased skeletal muscle GLUT4 expression, enhanced fatty acid oxidation, mitochondrial biogenesis (via PGC-1α), and improved insulin sensitivity. In cardiac ischemia-reperfusion models, AMPK activation by AICAR has been studied for protective effects on cardiomyocyte survival and energy restoration. In oncology research, AICAR's AMPK-activating and adenosine kinase-inhibiting properties have been studied in cancer cell line and xenograft models.
