## Compound Profile

Adipotide (CKGGRAKDC-GG-D(KLAKLAK)₂) is a synthetic proapoptotic peptidomimetic designed with a bifunctional architecture: a targeting domain (CKGGRAKDC) that homes to prohibitin on the vascular endothelium of white adipose tissue, linked via a GG spacer to a killing domain (D(KLAKLAK)₂) that disrupts mitochondrial membranes and induces apoptosis in targeted cells. The rationale behind adipotide is selective destruction of the vasculature feeding white adipose tissue deposits — reducing blood supply and inducing adipocyte death through ischemia, without systemic effects on other tissues that do not express the target receptor. Preclinical research in non-human primates (rhesus macaques) and rodent obesity models has examined adipotide's effects on body weight, fat mass distribution, and metabolic parameters. The prohibitin-targeting component was identified through phage display peptide library screening of white adipose vasculature, and adipotide represents the lead compound from that work. Research findings in non-human primates documented significant fat mass reduction, particularly in visceral adipose depots, with effects on renal tubular cells as the primary documented off-target toxicity — a research finding of significant mechanistic interest for understanding prohibitin expression in kidney versus adipose tissue.
