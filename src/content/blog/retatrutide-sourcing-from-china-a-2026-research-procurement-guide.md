## Seven questions to settle before ordering

A strong procurement request is specific enough to produce a useful answer. Before paying a final invoice, record the following in writing.

### 1. What material is being requested?

Use the exact product name and requested form. If your laboratory has specification requirements, state them before availability is checked. Do not assume that two listings with the same common name are identical in form, counterion, content, packaging, or documentation.

### 2. What quantity is needed?

Peptides of China’s standard catalog minimum order is USD 600. Different standard catalog items may be combined to reach that total, and orders normally begin with one 10-vial kit per product. For the current listing, review the [Retatrutide research catalog page](/catalog/retatrutide/).

### 3. Is the requested material currently available?

Availability should be confirmed for the actual request rather than inferred from a generic page. Peptides of China checks physical availability and reserve status during manual processing before the final invoice is issued.

### 4. What documentation is available for the proposed batch?

Documentation varies by product, manufacturer, and batch. Ask what exists for the material being proposed instead of assuming that every shipment includes a COA, HPLC chromatogram, LC-MS result, or a universal purity statement.

A document is most useful when the buyer can connect it to the proposed or delivered material through relevant identifiers. A polished PDF without a meaningful batch relationship is not a substitute for traceability.

### 5. What will the receiving laboratory verify?

Manufacturer-side documentation and buyer-side testing answer different questions. A laboratory may choose to arrange independent analysis under its own protocol after receiving the material. The scope, acceptance criteria, sample handling, and release decision belong to the buyer’s quality system.

For a practical overview of analytical evidence, see [Peptide Testing and Analysis](/blog/peptide-testing-and-analysis-a-professional-guide-to-laboratory-verification/) and Peptides of China’s current [Peptide Quality](/peptide-quality/) information.

### 6. What are the logistics and commercial terms?

Confirm the shipping method, destination-specific price, expected dispatch and transit window, and any information the receiving party needs before shipment. These details can vary by destination and available route, so they should be confirmed for the order rather than copied from a general estimate.

### 7. What happens if the proposal does not match the request?

Resolve specification questions before the final invoice. If something delivered appears inconsistent with the written order, preserve packaging and identifiers, document the issue promptly, and follow the current quality or support process. Avoid relying on promises that are not part of the written commercial terms.

## How to evaluate quality claims without overreading them

Research procurement improves when every claim is tied to a specific question.

- **Identity:** Does the evidence support that the material is the requested compound?
- **Chromatographic purity:** What method and integration support the reported relative purity?
- **Content:** How much target material is present in the supplied unit?
- **Form:** Which salt, counterion, or other material form is being supplied?
- **Batch relationship:** Can the document be connected to the proposed or received material?
- **Additional attributes:** Does the intended research require water content, residual solvent, bioburden, endotoxin, stability, or other tests?

HPLC, mass spectrometry, content assays, and other methods provide different pieces of evidence. No single percentage answers every quality question. The appropriate test panel depends on the material and the receiving laboratory’s intended research.

That is also why Peptides of China does not need to fill an article with a universal “99%+” promise. A better conversation is: what specification is requested, what evidence is available for the proposed material, and what will the buyer independently confirm?
