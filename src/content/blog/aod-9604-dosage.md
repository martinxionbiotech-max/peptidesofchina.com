## What Is AOD 9604

AOD 9604 is a modified fragment of human growth hormone, specifically the **176–191 region**.

Unlike full GH, it is designed to:

- stimulate lipolysis (fat breakdown)
- inhibit lipogenesis (fat formation)

Importantly, it does **not significantly activate IGF-1 pathways**, which differentiates it from traditional growth hormone.

## AOD 9604 Dosage (Short Answer)

Commonly referenced daily ranges:

- **250–500 mcg per day** → baseline range
- **500–1000 mcg per day (0.5–1 mg)** → upper commonly used range

These are typically discussed as **total daily exposure**.

## Injectable AOD 9604 Dosage

In research and practitioner-referenced contexts:

## Oral AOD 9604 Dosage

AOD 9604 has also been studied in oral form.

Because oral bioavailability is lower, higher amounts are typically referenced:

- in some contexts up to **2 mg/day**

Important:

Oral effectiveness depends heavily on formulation and delivery system.

## What Clinical Research Shows

AOD 9604 has been evaluated in human studies for fat loss.

Findings include:

- stimulation of fat metabolism
- reduction in adipose tissue in some study groups
- minimal impact on glucose metabolism and IGF-1

However:

Results across trials are mixed, and effects are generally **modest compared to GLP-1 class compounds**.

## Why Dosing Is Relatively Low

AOD 9604 acts as a **signaling fragment**, not a hormone replacement.

It influences:

- fat metabolism pathways
- enzyme activity related to lipolysis

Because of this:

- effective doses are in micrograms
- increasing dose does not guarantee stronger results
- effects depend heavily on metabolic context

## What AOD 9604 Does NOT Do

It is important to understand:

- it does not significantly increase IGF-1
- it does not produce rapid fat loss on its own

Its effects are:

- gradual
