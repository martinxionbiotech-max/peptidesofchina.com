For researchers and resellers sourcing **chinese peptides**, the practical questions are the same regardless of compound: who manufactured the vial, what the batch documentation shows, and whether the specification matches the listing. The practical starting point for a bulk research order is the quantity your project needs, the supplier fulfilling it and the total goods value for that supplier. Shipping is separate. Combining unrelated supplier totals can make a request look large enough while leaving an individual supplier order below its minimum.

This guide explains Peptides of China's current public-catalog rules and shows how to budget a request without confusing the goods minimum, first-order trial, deposit and final payment. The examples are illustrative calculations, not quotations or promises of stock availability.

## The current public-catalog rules

As described in the [Peptides of China FAQ](/faq/):

- The minimum is $600 in goods per supplier order. Shipping does not count toward it.
- An eligible new buyer can place one supplier order below $600 as a first-order trial.
- Only one supplier order in a request can use that trial. Other supplier orders must meet $600 or be removed.
- A 5% discount applies to the goods subtotal when that supplier's gross goods subtotal reaches $3,500. Supplier totals are not combined and shipping is excluded.
- Orders already created retain the terms under which they were placed. Pharma and pre-orders are outside these public-catalog rules.

Use the current catalog and request process to check the offered pack configuration and quantities. A minimum goods value does not mean every product is available individually or in every quantity.

## Calculate the minimum supplier by supplier

Suppose a returning buyer selects $220 in goods from Supplier A and $180 from Supplier B. The request contains $400 in goods overall, but neither supplier order reaches $600. Shipping charges do not fix that shortfall.

For the same buyer, $340 from Supplier A and $310 from Supplier B meet the goods-value minimum separately. Delivery costs remain additional and are confirmed through the request process.

The practical consequence is to group the request by supplier before deciding whether it meets the minimum. If a supplier subtotal is too low, consider whether the project genuinely needs another catalog item from that supplier. Do not add unnecessary material simply to make the arithmetic work. Removing or postponing that supplier order can be the better procurement decision.

## Understand the first-order trial

The first-order exception is intended for a new buyer with no fully paid ordinary public-catalog order. It applies to one supplier order, not to every supplier in a first request.

For example, an eligible buyer could use the trial for $180 in goods from Supplier A while ordering $320 from Supplier B. A second $150 supplier order would not automatically receive another exception.

The trial is held while the qualifying order remains active. It is used when that order becomes fully paid. If the order is cancelled before full payment, the trial is released even if a deposit was paid. These details matter when a buyer has an earlier active request: “this is my first delivery” and “I have no fully paid ordinary public-catalog order” are not interchangeable criteria.

The request process applies the eligibility rules. If an existing request or payment makes the displayed result unclear, resolve that question before opening duplicate requests.

## Apply the $3,500 discount threshold correctly

The threshold uses each supplier's gross goods subtotal before the 5% discount. At exactly $3,500, the discount is $175 and the discounted goods subtotal is $3,325. Shipping remains separate.

Two supplier orders of $1,800 each total $3,600 across the request, but neither reaches $3,500 individually. They do not earn this discount simply because their combined value exceeds the threshold.

Similarly, $3,450 in goods plus $75 shipping does not meet a $3,500 goods threshold. The shipping payment is a real budget cost, but it is not eligible goods value.

Use these examples to understand the published rule, not to predict unconfirmed combinations with other pricing arrangements. Review the actual quote and final invoice for the applicable prices and any agreed adjustments.

## Keep the deposit separate from the order minimum

Peptides of China's process begins with a catalog request and a $10 processing deposit, followed by manual review of availability and terms. The deposit is credited toward the final invoice; it is not an additional $10 charge on top of the same invoice amount.

Paying a deposit does not mean the final order is paid or that every requested product has been confirmed. Review the available quantities, delivery details and final invoice before completing payment. The current workflow is described in [How It Works](/how-it-works/).

In your own procurement records, distinguish the request submitted, deposit paid, confirmed terms, final payment and receipt. Treating these as separate stages prevents a submitted request from being mistaken for fulfilled purchasing.

## Budget for the complete project

The goods minimum is one commercial condition. A practical research budget may also need to account for delivery, destination-specific charges where applicable, analytical testing and the resources needed to receive and document the material.

Prepare the product list around the intended work:

1. Identify the exact compound and chemical form required, rather than relying on a similar product name.
2. Check the currently offered pack configuration and supplier.
3. Calculate each supplier's goods subtotal independently.
4. Check first-order eligibility or the discount threshold where relevant.
5. Review shipping and the confirmed order terms separately.
6. Decide what documentation and testing the research requires.

A larger purchase does not establish a better analytical result. Product identity, impurity profile and peptide content remain separate questions. Choose the documentation and measurements appropriate to the project rather than treating an order threshold as a quality certification.

## Questions before submitting

### Can several products from one supplier contribute to the minimum?

The minimum is based on that supplier order's goods value. Select the available catalog configurations and review the resulting supplier subtotal in the request process.

### Can I combine different suppliers to reach $600 or $3,500?

No. Both thresholds are assessed per supplier order, not across the entire request.

### Does shipping count?

No. Shipping is excluded from both the goods minimum and the discount threshold.

### Is every supplier in my first request exempt?

No. An eligible buyer can use the first-order trial for one supplier order. Other supplier orders must meet the normal minimum or be removed.

### Do the new rules change an order I already created?

The published FAQ says existing orders retain their original terms. Check the actual order record rather than recalculating it using a later article or announcement.
