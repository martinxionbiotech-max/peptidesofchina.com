## Start with an accurate document set

- Use a commercial invoice and packing information that match the material, quantity, sender, recipient, and declared value.
- Include a safety data sheet or other document when required for the material and destination.
- Keep the batch or lot identifier consistent across the label, order record, and any analytical document supplied for review.
- State the research-only purpose accurately and do not describe a research material as an approved medicine or consumer product.

## Classification and destination review

Chemical classification, import forms, duties, and exemptions depend on the destination, material, and current rules. Do not assume that a single HS code, TSCA statement, FDA position, or EU rule applies to every peptide or jurisdiction. Before dispatch, the buyer should confirm the current requirements with the relevant customs authority or a qualified customs broker.

## Avoid concealment and documentation mismatch

“Stealth shipping,” mis-manifesting, undervaluing, and labeling a material as something else create avoidable compliance and traceability risks. Neutral outer packaging, where permitted, does not replace accurate declarations or required documents. A direct sourcing route cannot guarantee customs clearance.
