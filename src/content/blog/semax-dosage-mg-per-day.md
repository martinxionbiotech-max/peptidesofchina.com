## Why Reconstitution Matters

With lyophilized powder, you are not working directly with volume — only with **total milligrams in the vial**.

After reconstitution, dosing depends on **concentration (mg/mL)**.

Example:

Vial: **5 mg Semax**
Diluent added: **5 mL**

→ Final concentration: **1 mg/mL**

This allows simple conversion:

- 0.3 mg = 0.3 mL
- 0.5 mg = 0.5 mL
- 1.0 mg = 1.0 mL

## Why Semax Works at Low Doses

Semax is not a stimulant or bulk-acting compound.

Because of this:

- it is active at low doses
- increasing dose does not produce linear effects
- excessive exposure may reduce efficiency or cause overstimulation
